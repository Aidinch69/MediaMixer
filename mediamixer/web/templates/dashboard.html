⚡ AnticipationExperimental

What does this do?

Tension builds (visual pulse, heartbeat, panned riser) when a jackpot is really incoming, with a tier reveal (small/big/mega) and a flash/chime/bass/floater-burst payoff + lingering afterglow when it lands. Milestone jackpot counts get an even bigger one. Near-misses play the same build-up with nothing behind it. Also enables occasional golden "bonus" tiles - rate one 👍 while it's glowing for a quick bonus - and rarer "sealed" tiles, dimmed and playing 25% slower while locked, where the reward itself (xp, a speed kick, a heat bump, or nothing) is only decided the moment you open it. Jackpots and bonuses earn XP toward a level that only ever goes up (top right), each one shifts the color theme, and a daily ~20min "Golden Hour" boosts the odds of all of the above if you happen to be around for it. Near-miss/bonus odds also creep up the longer a single session runs. The thin bar at the very top is an always-on charge meter; quiet tiles get a faint "due" aura; a build-up opens with a quick flash of the next color theme; mega/milestone jackpots can chain into a re-spin; and landing one gives you a few seconds to bank it or push your luck for a bonus (or lose the streak). Occasional "mystery tiles" blur and un-blur on their own with nothing behind them, just curiosity. A chase flash occasionally hops between tiles - tap it before it moves on to build a chain (missing it by a hair still gets acknowledged right on the tile, not just silently reset). Mega+ and milestone jackpots can also fade in a full-screen spiral for a while, at low opacity (sometimes gently breathing wider/narrower as it holds), pulled from a local Spirals/ folder. A small gold badge up top names the jackpot state itself (a build-up's tier, a live streak, or which Auto-Pilot zone is running) separately from the status bar's own rotating notifications just below it. Hearting tiles builds a heart multiplier that nudges the odds of all of the above (bigger jackpots, spirals, speed kicks, xp) a bit further - it decays if you stop, so it rewards staying engaged rather than one single heart early on. A rare "Color Rush" occasionally sweeps through - falling color streaks, a hue-cycling glow around the edge, every tile pulsing its own color on its own beat, and a confetti burst, all at once; smaller standalone pieces of the same effects (just confetti, just the tiles, etc.) can also show up on their own tied to a streak, an unlock, a bonus, or a jackpot. Every so often there's also a genuinely hidden window where rewards are secretly worth double - nothing announces it, you only find out from the "(hidden x2!)" tag on whatever you happened to win while it was running. Spin itself rolls visibly through its possible outcomes before landing, and can land "so close" to a jackpot and say so, instead of that almost-miss going unmentioned.
