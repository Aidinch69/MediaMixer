💬 Mantra TextYour own phrases drifting, flashing, and pulsing over everything.
What does this do?

Short phrases from your list appear over the whole screen - above the spiral, below the controls. Drift floats a phrase up and away somewhere on screen; Flash is a brief centered word; On the Beat pops one on a strong hit in the audio or a Rhythm Mode downbeat. While a spiral is up, phrases also slow into a centered "trance" breathe. Frequency is a baseline - it rises on its own during a spiral, a Surge, Auto-Pilot's climax, and a Finale's build and peak, and a burst of phrases rides along with mega jackpots.

🎬 Auto-PilotExperimentalScripts pace, odds, and content novelty through a repeating calm-to-climax arc.
What does this do?

Scripts pace, barrage/jackpot odds, floater count, and content novelty through a calm -> building -> climax -> cooldown arc, instead of those staying wherever you last set them. "Typical Pace" is a baseline, not a fixed timer - each zone actually extends itself for as long as you're genuinely engaged with it (up to a cap) and cuts short if you're not, so a real cycle can run noticeably shorter or longer than this number depending on how the session's actually going. Each cycle also picks a fresh "zone" (Chase/Color/Jackpot/Vault) that leans the climax toward a different mix of the passive stuff above - one cycle's climax might be chase-flash heavy, the next a Color Rush, the next sealed tiles - instead of every climax looking the same. Turns Floating Overlay on if it's off. Does not touch Mosaic Fit - that stays exactly wherever you set it manually. "Switch It Up" forces an immediate zone change plus a fresh novelty push, for whenever the satiation logic's own timing isn't fast enough for you.

🎞️ Dynamic MosaicExperimentalLets the tile-count layout change itself, zone-driven or ambient.
What does this do?

Lets the layout (tile count) change itself instead of only ever being a manual pick. Zone-Driven reads Auto-Pilot's current zone - each zone has its own hand-picked feel (Vault stays low/focused, Color and Jackpot go wide for spectacle) rather than one formula trying to guess a single right direction. Ambient Drift just wanders within your range on its own on a timer, with or without Auto-Pilot. Both stay inside the Tile Range below, and every change dips through the layout fade - and now respects a minimum cooldown between automatic changes so several can't land back to back.

🎆 FinaleOne planned build to a big climax, then an afterglow and a recap.
Ready.
What does this do?

Auto-Pilot's arc repeats calm → climax → cooldown for as long as it runs. A Finale is one longer climb that goes further than any single cycle, actually lands, and then ends. Build (first ~55%): pace quickens, jackpot odds and floaters climb, selection leans toward your favorites, playback speed eases up, mantras pick up, and Rhythm Mode joins in. Peak (to ~85%): the spiral fades in, a Surge runs the whole stretch, and color splashes and tile FX keep firing. Climax: a guaranteed MEGA jackpot, a full Color Rush, and a mantra flood all at once. Afterglow: everything eases below normal and settles, then a recap of the session. While it runs it takes over the controls Auto-Pilot normally drives, then gives them back (or restores your manual settings if Auto-Pilot is off). With the offer option on, a long Auto-Pilot session suggests one once - ignoring it just lets it fade.

⚡ AnticipationExperimentalBuild-up/payoff FX, bonus & sealed tiles, chase flashes, jackpots, and more.
What does this do?

Tension builds (visual pulse, heartbeat, panned riser) when a jackpot is really incoming, with a tier reveal (small/big/mega) and a flash/chime/bass/floater-burst payoff + lingering afterglow when it lands. Milestone jackpot counts get an even bigger one. Near-misses play the same build-up with nothing behind it. Also enables occasional golden "bonus" tiles - rate one 👍 while it's glowing for a quick bonus - and rarer "sealed" tiles, dimmed and playing slower while locked, where the reward itself (xp, a speed kick, a heat bump, or nothing) is only decided the moment you open it. Jackpots and bonuses earn XP toward a level that only ever goes up (top right), each one shifts the color theme, and a daily ~20min "Golden Hour" boosts the odds of all of the above if you happen to be around for it. Near-miss/bonus odds also creep up the longer a single session runs. The thin bar at the very top is an always-on charge meter; quiet tiles get a faint "due" aura; a build-up opens with a quick flash of the next color theme; mega/milestone jackpots can chain into a re-spin; and landing one gives you a few seconds to bank it or push your luck for a bonus (or lose the streak). Occasional "mystery tiles" blur and un-blur on their own - most of the time with nothing behind them, just curiosity, but sometimes it's a genuine swap to something never shown before. A chase flash occasionally hops between tiles - tap it before it moves on to build a chain (missing it by a hair still gets acknowledged right on the tile, not just silently reset). Mega+ and milestone jackpots can also fade in a full-screen spiral for a while, at low opacity (sometimes gently breathing wider/narrower as it holds), pulled from a local Spirals/ folder. A small gold badge up top names the jackpot state itself (a build-up's tier, a live streak, or which Auto-Pilot zone is running) separately from the status bar's own rotating notifications just below it. Hearting tiles builds a heart multiplier that nudges the odds of all of the above (bigger jackpots, spirals, speed kicks, xp) a bit further - it decays if you stop, so it rewards staying engaged rather than one single heart early on. A rare "Color Rush" occasionally sweeps through - falling color streaks, a hue-cycling glow around the edge, every tile pulsing its own color on its own beat, and a confetti burst, all at once; smaller standalone pieces of the same effects (just confetti, just the tiles, etc.) can also show up on their own tied to a streak, an unlock, a bonus, or a jackpot. Every so often there's also a genuinely hidden window where rewards are secretly worth double - nothing announces it, you only find out from the "(hidden x2!)" tag on whatever you happened to win while it was running. Spin itself rolls visibly through its possible outcomes before landing, and can land "so close" to a jackpot and say so, instead of that almost-miss going unmentioned. Rarely (at most every ~8 minutes, never on top of another event) a Spotlight Takeover grows one tile - often a favorite - to most of the screen for 15-20 seconds while the rest dim; hearting it then pays extra. The 🔥 Overdrive meter bottom-left fills as you heart, catch, and claim (hearts on a Rhythm Mode beat count double) and never drains on its own - release it (tap it or press O) whenever you want a peak: every tile speeds up, the odds of barrages and jackpots spike, a Surge and a spiral kick in, a favorite gets the Spotlight, and FX fire. Clip changes crossfade, zoom-wipe, or glitch-cut instead of hard cutting (mix and length in Tuning → Clip Transitions).

🏆 Progress & ChallengesLifetime bests, daily quests, and the trophy case.
What does this do?

Lifetime bests and two daily quests that re-roll each day and auto-claim XP on completion. The Weekly Arc is a longer chain that runs Monday to Sunday: its stages unlock one after another, each pays XP, and finishing all of them pays a bigger bonus - a different arc each week. The calendar marks every day you've opened MediaMixer in the last three weeks; coming back on consecutive days builds a visit streak with XP at 3, 7, 14, 30 days and beyond. Everything is stored on this device, nothing leaves it. The trophy case unlocks permanent badges as lifetime stats cross thresholds (hearts, jackpots, chase chains, prestige, Finales, weekly arcs, visit streaks, in-sync runs, Overdrive, Spotlight) - once earned, a trophy stays forever.

🌀 Visual FX RewardsPrism, kaleido, zoom, RGB split, VHS, and shockwave moments on tiles.
What does this do?

A new kind of thing to win: a few seconds of visual effect on the tile a reward landed on, which also pulls your eye to that video. They can ride along with a bonus tap, a sealed-tile open, or a chase catch (odds in Tuning → Visual FX Rewards); catch-streak milestones always get one; medium+ jackpots light up a couple of tiles; Spin has a new "FX Burst" result; and a long in-sync run in Rhythm Mode earns one too. Zoom Pulse kicks in time with the audio (or Rhythm Mode's beat) when there's something to follow.

🥁 Rhythm ModeA beat the grid moves to, tempo waves, and in-sync runs.
What does this do?

Each beat lights the next tile in a snake through the grid, and every downbeat (1 of 4) kicks the screen-edge glow. Tempo Waves speed the beat up over about 70 seconds, then drop back to base - and with Speed Follows Tempo on, every tile's playback speed rides the same wave (about 0.95x at base up to 1.25x at the top), so each wave builds and releases. Follow Audio tries to lock onto a steady beat in what's playing; spoken-word tracks often don't have one, so it falls back to your manual tempo and says so above. Every unbroken minute in sync earns XP (every other minute a tile FX too), and hearting a tile right on the beat charges Overdrive double. Auto-Pilot's new Rhythm Zone and a Finale's build/peak switch this on by themselves while they run.

🆕 Fresh DropsNew clips debut one at a time with a reveal, rationed per session.
What does this do?

Clips you add to media_assets after today are held back from normal rotation (and from the floaters and mystery tiles) until each gets its own debut: a tile dims under a "fresh drop" shimmer with a riser, then the new clip lands with a reveal. They're rationed so a new batch isn't burned through in one sitting - none in the first ~90 seconds, at least ~3 minutes apart, and at most 4 per session (all adjustable in Tuning → Fresh Drops); the rest wait for next time. New files are picked up within a couple of minutes, no reload needed. Once a clip has debuted it joins normal rotation, where being lightly played already earns it extra discovery weight for a while. Everything already in your library before this feature counts as old, not fresh.

What does this do?

Auto-Pilot's own arc blends this automatically while it's running (see the Auto-Pilot tab) and hands control back to this dial the moment you turn it off.

🔊 Device AudioOverlay track, rotation pace/tone, and this device's volume levels.
What does this do?

Volume (binaural/overlay/tile) is just for this device. Rotation pace and the beat/carrier are the one exception - those stay in sync with every device watching the same session. Tile Audio is every tile's own clip audio, all at once, at this one shared level - 0% keeps every tile muted (the default). There's no per-tile solo; turning it up means every visible tile plays together at the same volume.

🎚️ Audio-Reactive FXEdge glow and tile pulses that move with what you're hearing.
What does this do?

Listens to the mix of everything you can hear - the overlay track, plus every tile's own audio whenever Tile Audio is above 0% - and drives a soft screen-edge glow from the loudness, with a quick glow flash on a tile whenever there's a real hit (a jump above the recent baseline, not just loud audio). It's tapped after each volume slider, so it follows what's actually audible. The binaural tone and sound effects are deliberately left out: one is a steady drone that would flatten every reading, the other would make effects trigger off their own sounds. The same hits also drive "On the Beat" mantras, Zoom Pulse kicks, and Rhythm Mode's Follow Audio tempo.

🔧 TuningBackdoor

What does this do?

Live sliders for the constants we've actually been iterating on this session (spiral opacity/timing, sealed/chase/bonus/color-rush/mystery-tile/due-aura odds and timing, spin, the various multipliers and decay rates, xp amounts) - not everything in the file, just the parts worth fine-tuning in real time instead of editing source and restarting. A "check interval" is how often that system rolls the dice at all, in seconds - not a guarantee. A lower interval means it checks more often; the "odds" slider next to it is the actual chance it fires on each check. Low interval + low odds can feel about the same as high interval + high odds - they both control frequency, just from different angles. Changes persist across reloads on this device. "Save Backup" snapshots your current settings so you can experiment further and get back to them later; "Reset All to Shipped Defaults" always goes back to the values this shipped with, independent of any backup.

🖧 EngineLive

What does this do?

The engine's own settings from config.py - barrage/jackpot odds, the end-of-clip tension slow-down, and how the selection learns from hearts, skips, and locks. Unlike the sliders above, these live on the PC running the engine, so they apply to every device at once and take effect immediately, no restart. They're saved next to the engine (config_overrides.json) and survive restarts; a ● marks anything changed from config.py. When something feels right, "Copy Changed Values" gives you the lines to lock into config.py for good. Auto-Pilot, a Finale, and Overdrive still push barrage/jackpot odds around on their own while they run - these are the baseline they return to.
