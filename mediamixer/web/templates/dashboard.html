⚡ Anticipation (Experimental)

Tension builds (visual pulse, heartbeat, panned riser) when a jackpot is really incoming, with a tier reveal (small/big/mega) and a flash/chime/bass/floater-burst payoff + lingering afterglow when it lands. Milestone jackpot counts get an even bigger one. Near-misses play the same build-up with nothing behind it. Also enables occasional golden "bonus" tiles - rate one 👍 while it's glowing for a quick bonus. Jackpots and bonuses earn XP toward a level that only ever goes up (top right), each one shifts the color theme, and a daily ~20min "Golden Hour" boosts the odds of all of the above if you happen to be around for it. Near-miss/bonus odds also creep up the longer a single session runs. The thin bar at the very top is an always-on charge meter; quiet tiles get a faint "due" aura; a build-up opens with a quick flash of the next color theme; mega/milestone jackpots can chain into a re-spin; and landing one gives you a few seconds to bank it or push your luck for a bonus (or lose the streak). Occasional "mystery tiles" blur and un-blur on their own with nothing behind them, just curiosity. A chase flash occasionally hops between tiles - tap it before it moves on to build a chain. Mega+ and milestone jackpots can also fade in a full-screen spiral for a while, at low opacity, pulled from a local Spirals/ folder. A small gold badge up top names the jackpot state itself (a build-up's tier, or a live streak) separately from the status bar's own rotating notifications just below it.
